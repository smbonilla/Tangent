Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 September 2026

Tangent does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your diary entries. Your recordings, transcripts, profile, summaries, and insights stay on your device.

Information Tangent handles

Tangent handles the information you choose to give it: microphone audio while you record, diary transcripts, your name, interests and concerns, generated summaries and insights, app preferences, and an optional reminder time.

How your information is used

This information is used only to provide the app's diary, transcription, reminder, summary, and insight features. Speech transcription and optional AI processing happen on your device. Tangent has no account system, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Recordings and storage

Audio is written to a temporary file on your device while Tangent transcribes it. The temporary recording is removed after the complete transcript and diary entry have been saved. If recording is interrupted, it may remain on the device so that Tangent can recover your entry. Your diary data remains on your device unless you choose to export it.

Optional downloads

Apple speech language assets may be downloaded when they are needed. If you choose to use AI features, Tangent downloads the model you select from Hugging Face. Your diary content is not included in these requests. Apple and Hugging Face may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

Tangent asks for microphone access so you can record an entry. If you turn on a daily reminder, it also asks for notification permission. You can change either permission in your device settings.

Exports and deletion

An export leaves Tangent only when you choose where to send or save it. You can remove downloaded AI models from Settings. Removing the app removes its locally stored app data, though copies may remain in device backups that you control.

This website

This website does not set cookies or include analytics. Its hosting provider may process standard request logs needed to deliver the site.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy if Tangent changes. The date above will show the latest revision. For privacy questions, email magnusaross@gmail.com.
